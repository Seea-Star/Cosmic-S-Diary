Pumpkin Tiramisu & Bright Sun

At school, we prepared single-portion pumpkin tiramisu in small glass bowls for a local shop opening. Here is how we built it step by step:

1. Soft pumpkin cream base
2. Soaked ladyfinger
3. More pumpkin cream
4. Dark chocolate ganache
5. Final pumpkin cream dollops on top! 🎃✨
